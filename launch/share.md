## Order of operations on launch day

1. Post the main tweet with `images/hero-x.png` (options in `x-posts.md`).
2. Publish the article, quote-post it an hour later with the `111k` cover.
3. Then drop the group messages above. Groups first kills the post's reach — the algorithm
   wants engagement on the post, not link clicks from elsewhere.
